What "cash to you at closing" includes. The sale price, minus commission on both sides and the 13% HST on those commissions, minus the mortgage payout, any penalty, liens, legal fees, and disbursements, minus any credits the buyer asked for, plus or minus closing adjustments, minus carrying costs for the days you stay after closing.

Costs you already paid to prepare the sale, such as staging, photos, and repairs, are not deducted from closing cash because you paid them out of pocket. They appear in "after prep costs," which shows what you actually keep.

Land transfer tax is paid by the buyer, so it is not included. Any tax on the sale depends on your situation, including whether the property was your principal residence, so check with an accountant. This tool is an estimate, not legal or tax advice.
